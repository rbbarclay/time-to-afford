Keep in mind

  • Colors group metros into five groups of about equal size, from fewest to most hours. They show rank, not equal steps in hours; the legend gives each group's range.
  • With no wage entered, each metro uses its own median wage.
  • Grocery prices are estimates. Scenario figures (the grocery basket and the mortgage payment) aren't mapped; see them on the main page.
  • Hollow dots have no value: not covered by the source, no data, or top-coded.
