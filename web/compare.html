Keep in mind

  • With no wage entered, each metro uses its own median wage, so the hours compare a typical worker in each place, not the same person.
  • The difference is the second metro minus the first, in hours of work, and as a percentage of the first. It's left blank when either metro has no value.
  • Grocery prices are estimates, and the basket and mortgage payment are scenarios; see each row's “How is this calculated?”.
