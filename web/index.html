    Keep in mind

    • Wages are pre-tax; take-home pay buys less.
    • Sources cover different periods: ….
    • Gas prices exist for only 10 metros; EIA's areas may not match metro boundaries exactly.
    • Local grocery prices are estimates: the national average price adjusted by the metro's price level for all goods (BEA), since no food-specific local index exists. Puerto Rico metros have none.
    • The mortgage payment is a scenario from the rate and down payment you choose. It leaves out property taxes and insurance; the typical owner costs figure includes them.
    • Rents are survey estimates (with margins of error) and include utilities.
    • A metro median wage is not any one person's pay.
